About

House and garage with weight behind it. Henry Hamilton plays the kind of records that fill a floor slowly and keep it there: warm, groove led, built for the long set.

Raised between Essex and East London, Henry came up through the Dance On Arrival family and has since taken the sound from Studio 338 to the beach clubs of Phuket. Alongside the sets he produces his own music, with his first mix series, Every Sound Has Roots, out now on SoundCloud and a run of unreleased originals on the way.
