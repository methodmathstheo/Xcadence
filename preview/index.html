About

House and tech house with weight behind it. Henry plays the kind of records that fill a floor slowly and keep it there: warm, groove led, built for the long set.

Raised between Essex and East London, Henry came up through the Dance On Arrival family and has taken the sound from Studio 338 to the beach clubs of Phuket, with a Circoloco showcase and a B2B with Kodie along the way.

Away from the booth he produces his own music. His first mix series, Every Sound Has Roots, is out now on SoundCloud, with a run of unreleased originals (Gimme Sum, Don't Go, Can You) on the way.
